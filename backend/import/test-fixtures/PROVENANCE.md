# USDA test fixture provenance

`foundation.json` is a reduced excerpt of two real records from the USDA
FoodData Central Foundation Foods JSON release dated 2026-10-29, published under
CC0 1.0. Official download:
<https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_foundation_food_json_2026-10-29.zip>.
Nutrients and portions are intentionally truncated.

`branded.json` is synthetic, not USDA data. The record mirrors the official
BrandedFoods shape; its `gtinUpc` value `001633636543505` is fabricated and
deliberately contains leading zeros.
